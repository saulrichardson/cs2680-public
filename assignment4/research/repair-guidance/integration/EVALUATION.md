# Evaluate the guidance as an intervention

The quality of a document and the quality of the decisions it induces are related but distinct. A clear explanation can be technically sound and still be ignored, misapplied, or overwhelmed by another interpretation. Evaluate both the delivered material and the investigation that follows it. A structural validator or successful scripted flow cannot establish repair improvement.

## Preserve a usable control

Freeze the original task, starting source, model route and sampling conditions, tools, operating instructions, dependency environment, grader, and real external limits. Compare the existing foundation with that same foundation plus the guidance under study. If a replacement foundation is itself the intervention, describe it explicitly; do not attribute its result solely to added domain lessons.

Use fresh attempts rather than continuing a solver that already saw the answer or grade. Keep the golden patch and author transcript privileged to the authoring or human-analysis stage. A same-task experiment tests whether a lesson helps the task it was derived from. A separate held-out task is needed to examine transfer. Never choose only new successes when constructing the comparison or omit later regressions from the cohort.

A small initial comparison can include prior regressions, prior gains, and representative persistent failures. Preserve its selection before execution. Repeated paired trials help distinguish stochastic variation from a stable effect; a single historical comparison does not provide a precise estimate of general improvement. Keep execution order and environment effects visible when they could matter.

## Measure the claim actually being made

For a first-attempt claim, record the first candidate sent to the original grader before its feedback is used. Local edits and self-tests may legitimately occur before that submission. Record final outcomes separately, since a pass after feedback does not establish a first-candidate improvement. Preserve the relationship between captured, submitted, and graded bytes, and separate selection-provenance errors from the original outcome for actual submitted bytes.

Keep setup failure, compilation failure, assertion failure, interrupted work, unknown effects, and semantic repair failure distinct. A large required-failure count can result from a single build blocker or from a task where no model ever started. These cases remain part of the end-to-end result but should not be narrated as many independent reasoning errors.

Record time and known provider usage with their actual scope. Different generated work makes cost or latency a useful operational observation without proving an isolated efficiency mechanism. Retain unknown or aborted charges as unknown rather than treating them as zero.

## Follow the lesson into a decision

Identify the point where the guidance was meant to help. Did the investigator read the relevant body? Did it inspect the actual caller, preserve a metadata assertion, choose the real setter, check the specified signature, or distinguish a new absent result? Did that observation change the source or the theory? A mention of the skill in a summary does not establish that the technique was used.

For an unread skill, distinguish discovery failure from a skill that was read but not applied. For correct advice that was ignored, adding another sentence of the same advice may not address the problem. A focused example or a different placement may be a better hypothesis, but it needs another behavioral test. If a technically wrong claim drove an action, correct and verify that claim before attributing the failure to general model limitations.

Inspect successes with the same care as failures. A new pass may result from avoiding accidental cache destruction, unrelated stochastic choices, or a better implemented contract. Preserve promising explanations as hypotheses and identify the evidence that would distinguish them. Do not treat every successful trajectory as proof that all supplied documents helped.

## Validate authoring conditions

An author should have an environment that can execute the behavior its technical guidance depends on. A generic source checkout may be enough to study architecture but not to validate a framework's scheduler, native type, serializer, or import behavior. Record whether a probe reached its assertions or failed at dependency setup. A pipeline's zero exit can conceal a missing executable.

Verify the reference solution under the same relevant conditions before using it as a source of expected behavior where feasible. Treat its incidental implementation choices as examples rather than requirements. The author should identify observations available without that solution and explain why they would distinguish a tempting wrong repair. Preserve uncertainty in the solver-facing guidance when it matters, rather than leaving it only in a human analysis the solver will not see.

## Improve the publication from observed use

Revise a chapter when evidence identifies a missing condition, misleading example, unsupported technical assertion, or unhelpful route to the relevant material. Preserve its depth and exceptions rather than accumulating unrelated warnings at the end. A small correction that changes a consequential action can be more valuable than a larger catalog.

A reasonable promotion decision considers original grades, regressions, first-candidate results, actual skill use, candidate identity, and the mechanism visible in traces. The host retains its own judgment and lifecycle. This evaluation method adds no mandatory planning, review, testing, or finishing gate to a solver's ordinary work.
